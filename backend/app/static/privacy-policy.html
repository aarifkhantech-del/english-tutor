Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026  ·  Last updated: 19 August 2026

VocalBharat (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an AI spoken English tutor for Hindi speakers. You speak in Hindi on the web app or the Android app; we transcribe your speech, translate it into natural English, explain grammar in simple Hindi, and play pronunciation audio so you can practise. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Who this policy covers

It applies to:

  • the website and web tutor at english-tutor-6fx2.onrender.com
  • the VocalBharat Android app (com.vocalbharat.app)
  • related services such as Google Sign-In, optional email OTP login, subscriptions, help, and feedback

We operate from India and primarily serve learners in India. If you use VocalBharat from another country, your information may be processed in India and in the countries where our hosting and AI providers run their systems.

2. Information we collect

Account and login

  • Google account email — used as your account identifier when you choose Google Sign-In. We receive only the verified email needed to create or access your account.
  • Email address — if you choose the optional email login, we send a one-time 6-digit OTP. We do not store a password.
  • OTP records — we store a hashed OTP, expiry time, and attempt counts. We do not store the OTP in plain text.
  • Session token — after login, a JWT is stored in your browser (localStorage) or on the Android device so you stay signed in.
  • Usage counters — number of tutor/grammar requests, last login time, and account creation time, so we can enforce the free-tier quota and Pro plan.

Speech, practice, and learning content

  • Microphone audio — when you tap the mic, speech is captured so we can turn Hindi (or Hinglish) into text. On the web app, the recording is sent to our servers and transcribed in memory; we do not save recordings as audio files. On the Android app, transcription uses the device speech recognizer (which may involve Google’s speech services under Google’s policies); we then send the confirmed Hindi text to our API, not a stored audio clip.
  • Transcribed text — the Hindi text produced from your speech, plus any Hindi text you type and confirm.
  • Tutor output — English translation, grammar notes, and generated pronunciation audio returned to your device for that session.
  • Speak History (Android) — past Hindi→English practice items are saved in the app’s private storage on your phone. We do not upload this history as a separate archive to our servers.

Server logs may briefly include transcribed practice text and technical errors so we can keep the tutor working. Logs are not used to build advertising profiles.

Payments and subscriptions

  • Plan status — free vs monthly Pro, start/expiry dates, and request quota.
  • Payment records — amount, currency (INR), status, and gateway order/payment identifiers from Razorpay (or another gateway we configure).

Card numbers, UPI PINs, and other payment secrets are collected by the payment provider (currently Razorpay Checkout), not by VocalBharat. We never ask you to email card details.

Help and feedback

If you submit Feedback or Help, we collect the name, email, rating or issue type, message, and optional device/app version you provide, and we email that to our support inbox so we can reply.

Technical data

Our hosting provider may automatically receive IP address, browser or app type, and request timestamps in standard server logs. We do not run third-party advertising SDKs or sell your data.

3. How we use information

  • provide Hindi-to-English tutoring, pronunciation audio, and Grammar Explorer
  • authenticate you with Google Sign-In or optional email OTP and remember your Pro plan across devices
  • enforce free-tier limits and activate paid plans after successful payment
  • answer support tickets and improve reliability (for example, fixing failed transcriptions)
  • comply with law, prevent abuse, and protect the service

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your speech to train public advertising models, and we do not show third-party ads in the app.

4. Permissions (Android)

  • Microphone — required to practise speaking. You can refuse or later revoke it in Android Settings; speaking features will not work without it.
  • Internet — required to reach the VocalBharat API, Google Sign-In, AI tutoring, OTP email, and payments.

5. Third-party processors

We share only what is needed to run the product:

  • Mistral AI — receives the Hindi/English text of a practice or grammar request so the tutor can translate and explain. Audio files are not sent to Mistral; text is.
  • Google — Google Sign-In processes authentication according to Google’s policies. If you use the Android mic, the system speech recognizer may also process audio according to Google’s and your device’s privacy settings.
  • Razorpay — processes Pro payments and may collect billing information under Razorpay’s own privacy policy.
  • Email / SMTP provider — delivers OTPs and forwards help/feedback messages.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores accounts, hashed OTPs, subscriptions, and payment metadata.
  • Hosting (currently Render) — runs the web app and API.

Those providers process data under their terms. We ask them only to process data for VocalBharat, not to use it for unrelated marketing.

6. Retention

  • Account, quota, and subscription records — kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for billing disputes and legal requirements.
  • OTP hashes — short-lived (the code itself expires in minutes).
  • Audio — processed in memory for transcription and not stored as a media library.
  • Speak History — stays on your Android device until you delete entries or clear history in the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Help/feedback emails — retained as needed to resolve your request.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law (including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may request:

  • access to the personal data we hold about your email account
  • correction of inaccurate account details
  • deletion of your account and associated subscription records, subject to lawful retention (for example payment records)
  • withdrawal of consent for optional processing (for example, stop using the mic or sign out)

To exercise these rights, email vocalbharat91@gmail.com from the same address you use to sign in. You can also sign out in the app, clear Speak History on Android, and revoke microphone permission at any time.

8. Children

VocalBharat is an education product for learners practising spoken English. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for children. Under Indian law, a parent or lawful guardian should consent where the user is a child. If you believe a child has given us personal data without appropriate consent, contact us and we will delete it where required.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed OTPs, and access-controlled databases. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Do not share OTP codes. If you suspect unauthorised access to your email, contact us and change your email-provider password.

10. International users

By using VocalBharat you understand that your information may be transferred to India and to countries where our processors operate. If those laws differ from your home country, we still apply the practices described here.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the product or the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes that affect how we use personal data will be highlighted on this page.
