Tenses (Past, Present, Future)

English Definition

Tenses indicate the time of an action or state of being. They show whether something happens in the present, happened in the past, or will happen in the future.

Hindi Explanation (सरल भाषा में)

Tense यानी 'काल' यह बताता है कि कोई क्रिया किस समय में हुई - भूतकाल (Past), वर्तमान काल (Present), या भविष्य काल (Future)।
